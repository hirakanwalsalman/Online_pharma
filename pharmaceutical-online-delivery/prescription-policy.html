Prescription Policy

Prescription-only medicines require a valid prescription and pharmacist review. Uploading a file does not guarantee approval or confirm that a medicine can be dispensed. This demonstration illustrates a workflow and is not a pharmacy or a substitute for professional care.

How prescription review works

  1. Select Upload Prescription on a prescription-only product.
  2. Provide your contact details and upload a clear JPG, PNG or PDF file (up to 5 MB).
  3. A pharmacist reviews the prescription and requested medicine. You can check the review status from the product listing.
  4. If approved, add that medicine to your cart and continue to checkout. Items awaiting review or declined cannot be checked out.

Handling your prescription

Prescription files are stored in a private bucket and are available only to authorized administrator accounts. In a live service, access must be limited to the qualified pharmacist responsible for review. Provide a valid prescription belonging to the intended patient. If more information is required, submit an updated prescription for review.

Medical safety

Never share another person's prescription without authorization. Do not change or stop a medicine based on website content; consult your prescriber or pharmacist. For urgent symptoms, seek appropriate medical care.
