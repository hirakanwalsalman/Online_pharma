Information in this demo

The checkout form sends your name, email, phone number and delivery address to the connected Supabase project. A prescription review request sends your name, email and phone number and uploads the selected prescription file to its private storage bucket. Review status is saved in this browser so you can check it later. Processing, hosting location and retention depend on the connected Supabase project configuration.

Health and prescription information

This is a demonstration, not an operating pharmacy or verified clinical service. Do not upload real prescriptions or provide real health information. The demonstration does not provide a complete privacy process for sensitive information.
